# Real offers and an optional introduction

Visit uses a separate authenticated owner avatar. Login grants control of that avatar; it does not make Lumi recognize a person, notice an item or remember a meeting. Her small foundational creator premise is separate from lived experience and contains no prior relationship, appearance, affection or trust.

## Offer food you actually possess

1. Enter **Visit**, walk to the shop and buy a permitted food item through the real checkout.
2. Carry it back and approach Lumi with a clear physical interaction opportunity.
3. Press **Offer held food** or **F** while the scene has focus.
4. Watch the actual offer status and your authoritative inventory. Use **Cancel offer** to withdraw a pending offer.

The button sends a request. Only the server can create a pending offer and reserve the actual owned item. A pending offer is not an accepted transfer, and an available sensory opportunity is not proof Lumi noticed it. The physical avatar, held item and offer pose are part of the same snapshot used by the ordinary viewer and her actual eyes.

She may accept, decline, defer or fail to notice. The local policy uses eligible attended evidence and present context; authenticated owner status is not a reason to accept. The owner cannot choose her response in the normal controls. An unnoticed expiry is reported separately from a deliberate decline, and no outcome supplies affection points or compulsory gratitude.

The prototype's explicit local policy ignores an offer when she cannot act or has an attended severe discomfort cue, defers while committed to another activity or without free hands, accepts with a current attended moderate/severe hunger cue and capacity, and otherwise declines. These are engineered present-context choices, not a learned preference. An unnoticed offer has no selected response. Pending reservations expire after **20 world seconds**, so a paused experiment does not count wall-clock waiting as elapsed offer time.

Acceptance transfers ownership exactly once after current reach, possession, reservation, item eligibility and capacity checks. It does not automatically consume the food or add nutrition. Any later eating is a separate validated production action. Decline, cancellation, expiry and disconnect release or retain inventory according to the recorded server outcome. A stale response cannot revive a terminal offer.

## Optionally display an introduction

While controlling Visit, use **Show introduction badge** to display the real in-world cue; use **Hide introduction badge** to remove it. The setting is explicit rather than a side effect of pairing or entering.

The public cue has a world-authenticated origin, but Lumi still needs a sufficiently visible, readable actual frame and appropriate attention. Darkness, occlusion, small size, inattention or an absent badge can prevent identification. A copied, unverified label is at most a perceived claim. A label in the owner's screen overlay is not evidence available to Lumi.

The authored starter recognizer is a narrow engineered perception rule, not general reading, learned familiarity or a successful VLM interpretation. Showing the badge does not guarantee a greeting, attention, successful encoding or an autobiographical meeting.

When an actual eligible introduction is attended and encoded, its original frame/evidence and timing support the first recognized encounter reference. An earlier anonymous encounter remains anonymous; later identification does not rewrite it. Connection logs, inventory transfer and the public activity timeline cannot create this experience.

## Observe, disconnect and recovery

Switching to Observe releases control and keeps the persistent avatar visibly parked. Pending offers have deterministic cancellation/release outcomes rather than silently transferring an item during disconnection. Avatar, item ownership, offer progress/results, introduction state and eligible encounter references are included in versioned checkpoint/replay state; authentication credentials and live sockets are not.

Use the existing diagnostic export and `npm run replay -- --bundle '<path>'` for offline causal reproduction without provider calls or live rendering. The normal recent timeline reports meaningful server actions and outcomes, with bounded retention. It is an owner-facing history, not Lumi's diary and not proof she witnessed everything in it.

Keep automated acceptance visits, offers and creator introductions in isolated EXPERIMENT or disposable test histories. Configuring owner access does not require placing an avatar in the owner's LIVE world or consuming the first real LIVE meeting as a smoke test.

Richer relationships, diary/social scoring, conversation, parcels and independent hosting remain outside Phase 2.5c.
