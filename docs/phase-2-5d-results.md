## Ordinary use

The existing page remains the entry point; Advanced diagnostics are unnecessary.

1. Observe Lumi's current home activities and their validated outcome history.
2. Pair only if needed, then explicitly **Enter Visit**. Use **W/A/S/D**, drag to
   look, and **E** for doors/checkout. This controls the owner's separate avatar.
3. Buy a permitted fruit or bread item with actual funds. Return to the small
   wall-mounted drop-off inside the home, approach with the held item, and press
   **Deposit held food · G**.
4. Read the separate requested/completed/rejected result. Storage full, reserved
   item, invalid reach, obstruction, stale authority or capacity failure preserves
   the rejected item. Depositing is not an in-person offer or a meal.
5. Switch to **Observe** or disconnect. The owner avatar remains visibly parked;
   it does not secretly move aside, disappear or become autonomous. The stored
   item remains. Refresh/re-entry uses the same eligible avatar and inventory.
6. Later actual perception can reveal an occupied indicator. Lumi can approach
   and open the container; a separate actual image can then reveal its contents.
   Collection and later validated eating remain distinct outcomes. She may defer
   or miss discovery; deposited does not mean noticed or accepted.

The normal card and compact timeline distinguish waiting, notice, opening,
collection, consumption, cancellation and failures. These are observer records,
not an automatic diary, private physiology readout or explanation of thoughts.
Timeline eviction/coverage limits remain visible. Observe without a visitor is
nonphysical. Authentication grants control only; it creates no identity,
attention, trust, greeting or memory.

## Storage, custody and perception

One designated drop-off holds **four nonexpiring fruit/bread items**. Successful
deposit moves custody from owner inventory into persistent world storage exactly
once. Logout, Observe, disconnect and normal recovery do not reclaim it. Opening
does not transfer ownership; collection transfers to Lumi, and consumption later
commits nutrition through the existing production action. Checkpointed cursors,
receipts and reservations prevent repeated transfers/effects after restore.

Terminal delivery receipts are bounded at16 and the observer timeline at32.
Active stored/collected items and references needed by offers, gift-use and
consumption are protected when older terminal records are pruned. The existing
town ledger remains bounded at8 items; no hidden spoilage, arbitrary placement,
shipping system or supply economy was added.

The shared scene contains the real box, lid, visible occupied indicator and
exposed food. Rendered geometry, sensor masks/occlusion, collision and reach use
that scene. Deposit supplies no cognitive notification or private known-item
flag. Occupied-container evidence does not reveal closed contents. Only current,
attended, immutable actual-RGB proof can supply delivery observations; encoding
is a separate capacity-checked gate. Transfers and connection records alone stay
outside `FOX_EXPERIENCE`.

There are **no sender labels** in this milestone. No hidden account identifier,
unreadable/absent sender, gift narrative, creator recognition, witnessed handover,
gratitude or affection is inferred from a parcel. Existing creator foundation,
first encounter and earlier anonymous experiences remain unchanged.

Inspection yields to committed basic care and present severe rest needs. A
missing fresh image has a bounded45-world-second inspection commitment rather
than indefinitely holding locomotion. Nominal deposit/parking poses admit an
alternative computed approach without requiring the owner to return and move.

Post-review corrections revalidate held-food capacity at the collection commit,
including concurrent in-person offer acceptance, so collection cannot replace a
newly accepted meal. Partial consume snapshots require their committed receipt
and matching gift-use cursor. CP11 migration and CP12 restore now validate the
full physical scene, including delivery fixtures; incompatible saved actors are
explicitly refused with the source preserved, never silently relocated. Opening
and closing the container validate endpoint actor clearance. Two fixed, back-set

readable from valid approaches. This changes real geometry/colliders, not RGB
recognition thresholds or camera-facing billboards.
